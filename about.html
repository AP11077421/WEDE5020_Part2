Our History

FORMEZ began in 2019, founded by Tokelo Mokoka and Homotsegang Mohlamonyane, and was officially put in stores in 2022. The founders are from Ntwane, a small village in Limpopo. Today, FORMEZ stands as South Africa's largest supplier of clothing and fashion.

Our Mission

FORMEZ's mission is to be the top fashion brand in South Africa, delivering trendy, seasonal collections that resonate with our customers.

Our Vision

Our vision is to use fashion to inspire, unite and create a sustainable future for the communities we serve.

Meet the Founders

  • Tokelo Mokoka – Co-Founder
  • Homotsegang Mohlamonyane – Co-Founder

Who We Serve

Our target audience includes people who want trendy, seasonal collections, as well as teenagers who are influenced by social media and current fashion trends.

About FORMEZ

Founded in 2019 by Tokelo Mokoka and Homotsegang Mohlamonyane, and officially launched in stores in 2022, FORMEZ began its journey in Ntwane, a small village in Limpopo. Today, FORMEZ stands as South Africa's largest supplier of clothing and fashion, known for trendy, seasonal collections that speak to a generation shaped by social media and self-expression.
